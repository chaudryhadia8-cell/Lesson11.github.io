How to make an Iced Matcha Latte!

Items Needed:

  • Bowl
  • Food Scale
  • Chasen Whisk
  • Water
  • Matcha Powder
  • Ice
  • Milk
  • A cup

Step 1: In a bowl, add 4g of matcha powder. Measure using food scale. Pour in 45g of hot water and whisk vigorously in a v or w motion until the matcha has fully incorporated into the water (around 30-40 sec). Try not to scrape the bottom on the bowl while you whisk.

Step 2: Fill your cup full of ice and add your milk of choice so the glass is about 3/4 of the way full.

Step 3: Lastly, pour your matcha into the glass, stir, and enjoy!
